04Beyond the code

What drives me

The thread through everything I do is building things people actually use. That is as true for a production AI system as it is for a student event with a room full of people counting on it.

The habit started before the code. At fifteen I was volunteering with kids who needed someone in their corner, and that grew into organizing events, co-founding a startup, and teaching. AI came later, and it stuck because it is the sharpest tool I have found for the same goal: take something painful and make it work for real people.

These days I split my time between shipping AI and helping run the communities I care about, and I have had the chance to represent my university and my country along the way. When I log off, I am usually watching Barça or arguing with my own World Cup model.
